    <div class="rules">

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>No Random Deathmatch (RDM)</strong>
            You must not kill or harm another player without proper reasoning.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)</strong>
            You must not use your vehicle to crash into others or disrupt roleplay.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>Roleplay Crashes</strong>
            All crashes must be roleplayed realistically. High-speed crashes should result in severe damage.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>No Intent To Roleplay (NITRP)</strong>
            Remain in character and do not troll within the server.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>Unrealistic Avatar</strong>
            Your avatar must resemble a real person. No unrealistic colors, floating items, or missing limbs.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>Cuff Rush & Auto Jail</strong>
            You must have a valid roleplay reason before cuffing someone and must roleplay the arrest properly.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>New Life Rule (NLR)</strong>
            After death, you may not return to the scene or seek revenge.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>Leaving To Avoid Punishment (LTAP)</strong>
            Leaving during staff interaction or detention to avoid punishment will result in a ban.
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <strong>Incorrect Livery / Uniform</strong>
            Only use liveries or uniforms you are authorized for. No non-UK or unauthorized designs.
        </div>

    </div>

    <div class="note">
        Please note that not all rules are listed above. If you are unsure about anything, feel free to contact a member of staff.
