            <div class="rule-title">Roleplay Crashes</div>

        <div class="rule">
            <div class="rule-title">No Intent To Roleplay (NITRP)</div>
            <div class="rule-desc">Remain in character at all times and do not troll within the server.</div>
        </div>

        <div class="rule">
            <div class="rule-title">Unrealistic Avatar</div>
            <div class="rule-desc">Your avatar must resemble a realistic person. No unrealistic colors, floating items, or missing limbs.</div>
